Cleaning Job & Payment Tracker for Excel

For recording cleaning jobs, partial payments, unpaid balances and overpayment credits.

  • Three sheets: Weekly Summary, Jobs and Payments, with prepared capacity for 500 jobs and 1,000 payments.
  • Weekly view of charges, applied payments, outstanding amounts, customer credits and overdue balances.
  • Warnings for missing charges, duplicate IDs and payments unmatched to a job.

Included: a blank Excel workbook, fictional worked example and printable quick-start guide.

Requirements and limits: Microsoft Excel is required. Google Sheets compatibility is not included in this version. Use one currency and update the review date before each weekly review.

No macros, account connections or monthly subscription are required. The tool does not process refunds, bank feeds, payroll or tax filings, and does not provide employee access permissions. Internal use within one business only; no resale or redistribution.

Cleaning Quote & Job Profit Calculator for Excel

For estimating the cost, suggested quote, profit and margin for one cleaning job using figures you enter.

  • Enter labor hours, labor cost per hour, supplies, travel, other costs and a target margin.
  • Calculate total estimated cost and a suggested quote rounded up to the next whole dollar.
  • Calculate estimated profit and margin on the rounded quote.

Included: a blank Excel calculator, fictional worked example and quick-start guide.

Requirements and limits: Microsoft Excel is required. Use one currency throughout and supply your own labor cost per hour. Results are estimates based on your entries, not guaranteed job outcomes. The calculator does not calculate sales tax, payroll or accounting entries.

Cleaning Weekly Job Planner for Excel

For organizing a Monday–Sunday cleaning schedule in one worksheet with 200 job rows.

  • Select a week using any date within it. Enter job dates, start and end times, cleaner or team, client, location, status and notes.
  • Calculate same-day durations and flag missing or invalid entries and overlapping active jobs assigned to the same cleaner or team.
  • Keep canceled jobs visible while excluding them from job counts and scheduled hours.

Included: a blank Excel workbook, fictional worked example and quick-start guide.

Requirements and limits: Microsoft Excel is required. Same-day appointments only. No reminders, calendar integrations, payroll or labor-compliance calculations.

Small Service Business Expense Log for Excel

For manually recording business expenses in 300 rows and reviewing monthly totals by category.

  • Enter the date, category, vendor, description, amount, payment method and receipt reference.
  • Review the selected-month total, valid-entry count and category subtotals.
  • Warnings identify incomplete or invalid entries.

Included: a blank Excel workbook, fictional worked example with input warnings and an HTML quick-start guide.

Requirements and limits: Microsoft Excel is required. Use one currency throughout. Receipt references are text; receipt files cannot be attached. Totals cover valid entered records and do not confirm that every expense has been captured.

One-time download, with no bank connection, accounting automation, tax-deductibility assessment or payroll features.
