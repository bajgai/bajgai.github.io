This is a small static site run by one person, Niranjan Bajgai, in Calgary, Alberta. Here is what it collects and why.

**Visit statistics.** The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count page views and see which pages are useful. Google sets cookies and records things like the pages you open, your rough location, device and browser. I only look at totals. There are no ads on this site. To opt out, block cookies for this site or install Google's [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Messages you send.** The contact form is delivered by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy), which passes your name, email and message to my inbox. I use them only to reply to you.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy) stores your email address. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.

**Bookings and payments.** Calls are booked through [Cal.com](https://cal.com/privacy) and payments go through [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy). I never see your full card number.

**Your data.** I do not sell or share your information with anyone else. Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) to ask what I hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.
