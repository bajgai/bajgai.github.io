<p class="eyebrow">What I automate</p>
<h2>The work that eats your week, handed to software</h2>
<p>Six areas where small service businesses lose the most hours. Most projects start with one of these and grow from there.</p>
</div>
<div class="automation-ledger">
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>7:40 PM · Lead calls</span>
<span>7:41 PM · Text sent → 8:05 PM · Booked</span>
</div>
<h3>Leads &amp; follow-up</h3>
<p>Missed calls texted back, web-form enquiries answered within minutes, and quiet leads followed up until they book or say no.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. missed-call text-back for a plumber</p>
</article>
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>9:00 AM · Inbox arrives</span>
<span>9:01 AM · Sorted → Reply drafted</span>
</div>
<h3>Inbox &amp; support</h3>
<p>Incoming email sorted and routed, replies to common questions drafted for you to approve, and requests turned into tickets.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. tenant requests that become work orders</p>
</article>
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>6:00 PM · Day closes</span>
<span>6:05 PM · Entries checked → Books updated</span>
</div>
<h3>Admin &amp; bookkeeping</h3>
<p>Receipts, invoices and payouts moved into QuickBooks or Xero, overdue-invoice reminders sent, and a plain summary at month end.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. Square sales posted to the books nightly</p>
</article>
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>3:10 PM · Visit ends</span>
<span>3:15 PM · Notes captured → Quote drafted</span>
</div>
<h3>Quotes &amp; documents</h3>
<p>Quotes, proposals, reports and letters drafted from a short form or a voice note, in your own format, ready for you to check and send.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. a site-visit voice note becomes a quote</p>
</article>
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>4:00 PM · Job finished</span>
<span>4:05 PM · Photos collected → Follow-up queued</span>
</div>
<h3>Content &amp; marketing</h3>
<p>Review requests after every job, Google Business posts, and social drafts made from the photos you already take on site.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. review request when a job is marked done</p>
</article>
<article class="ledger-entry">
<div class="event-log">
<span>8:00 AM · Work starts</span>
<span>8:01 AM · Tools synced → Dashboard ready</span>
</div>
<h3>Small apps &amp; dashboards</h3>
<p>A simple tool for when the spreadsheet stops being enough: job trackers, booking boards, reorder lists and one-page dashboards.</p>
<p class="trade-example">e.g. a reorder list for a restaurant kitchen</p>
</article>
</div>
