<p><strong>Total yes answers:</strong> ______ / 10</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>8 to 10:</strong> you're ready. Automate the task from question 1.</li>
  <li><strong>5 to 7:</strong> you're close. Fix the quick "no" answers (usually 3, 5 and 6), then start.</li>
  <li><strong>0 to 4:</strong> do the groundwork first. Write the process down, move it into digital tools and track the time it takes for two weeks.</li>
</ul>
