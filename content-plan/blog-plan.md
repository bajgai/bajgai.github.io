# Blog content plan: AI automation for small businesses

Working plan for bajgai.ca/blog, 30 days from 2026-10-17. Later threads add posts from this list in order and tick them off here.
This folder is excluded from the Jekyll build, so the plan is not published.

## Who the blog is for

Owners of small businesses (1 to 20 people) in Calgary, Alberta and the rest of Canada who lose time to admin and lose money to slow replies.
Wide net on purpose: trades and home services, clinics and wellness, restaurants and cafes, real estate and property management, retail and e-commerce, professional services, agencies.
They are not technical. They want to know what a thing does, what it costs in CAD and how fast it pays back.

## What every post does

- Solves one concrete problem the reader can recognise in their own week.
- Gives them something they can try today without hiring anyone.
- Ends with a short section that points to [bajgai.ca/services](https://bajgai.ca/services/): the free 20-minute discovery call first, then the $149 strategy session, $1,200+ builds or the $450/month care plan where they fit.
- Written in Niri's first-person voice, plain and direct, prices in CAD.
- Never invents client stories, results or numbers. Where a post needs Niri's real experience, leave an HTML comment `<!-- NIRI: ... -->` in the post (it does not show on the site) and keep the published text honest without it, for example by stating assumptions as assumptions.

## Front matter for a post

```yaml
---
layout: post
title: <title>
date: YYYY-MM-DD
description: <one sentence, shown on the blog list and in search results>
tags: <space separated, lower case, e.g. small-business automation calgary>
categories: <one of: how-to, local, examples, pricing>
---
```

File goes in `_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. Do not date a post in the future: the build skips future posts.
