Questions

Which tools do you work with?
Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zapier, Make, n8n, Airtable, Notion, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Stripe, Shopify and the OpenAI and Anthropic models, among others. If your tool has an API or an export, it can usually join in.
What happens to my data?
Automations run in accounts you own. I use only the access a workflow needs, and you can remove my access at any time.
What if the automation is not a fit?
You will hear that on the free call. If a build cannot do what the written scope says, I fix it or refund the deposit.
Do you work outside Calgary?
Yes. Everything happens by video call and screen share, anywhere in Canada or the United States.
How do I pay?
By card through Stripe, a secure checkout. Larger builds can be invoiced in two parts.
